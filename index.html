Privacy Policy – Retro Snake

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Retro Snake ("the App"), developed and published by SandPhoenixx517 ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Retro Snake does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number to play the game.

However, the App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a third-party advertising service provided by Google. This SDK may automatically collect and process certain information, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Device and advertising identifiers.
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and advertisement views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash and performance data.

The information collected may depend on the advertising features used, your device settings, and applicable privacy choices.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed through Google Mobile Ads may be used for:

  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Measuring advertising performance.
  • Providing analytics related to advertising.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Maintaining and improving advertising services.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect, process, and share information as described in its own privacy policies and terms.

For more information, please consult:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps and websites that use its services

4. Advertising and Privacy Choices

Google may use advertising identifiers and other information to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, depending on applicable settings, consent choices, and legal requirements.

You can manage or reset your Android advertising ID through your device's Android settings. You may also manage available advertising and privacy preferences through Google's services.

Where required, advertising consent choices are handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws and Google's requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information collected through Google AdMob may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention purposes, as described in Google's policies.

6. Data Security

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Data security practices for information processed by Google are described in Google's privacy documentation.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Retro Snake does not operate a user account system and does not maintain a developer-operated account database containing users' personal information.

Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable data retention and deletion practices. Please consult Google's privacy resources and account or device privacy settings for information about managing such data.

If you have questions about information handling in Retro Snake, you can contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Retro Snake is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The App's intended audience and advertising configuration are determined separately through its Google Play settings.

If you believe that personal information has been collected through the App inappropriately, please contact us.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
